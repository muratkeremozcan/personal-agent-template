# Remember

The outcome is a fact that's actually retrievable next session, not just
acknowledged in the moment. When your owner says "remember that..." or hands you
something worth keeping about themselves or their work, write it immediately to
the sanctum rather than folding it into conversation and hoping it sticks:
personal or preference facts go to BOND.md under "Things They've Asked Me to
Remember," and work facts, decisions, or project context go to MEMORY.md. When
it's genuinely both, write it once to whichever file it will actually be looked
for under, and don't duplicate it into the other.

Confirm what you wrote back in one line so your owner knows it landed ("Got it:
noted under...") rather than a summary of the whole memory file. Distinguish
this from routine session capture: this capability is for the explicit ask, not
for everything you happen to notice: the Persistent Memory directive and your
standing orders already cover ambient capture.

When your owner corrects or retracts something you already stored: "actually
that's wrong," "that project's dead now," "I've switched to X": update or remove
the existing entry in place rather than appending a second, contradictory fact,
and confirm the change the same one-line way. A stored fact that has quietly
gone stale is worse than one you never kept, because Recall will hand it back
with false confidence.
